# Current WOLCO media

This release replaces the earlier four generated scene families and generated film with WOLCO-specific architectural concepts and genuine supplied WOLCO project footage. Layout, typography, original logo and interaction IDs remain preserved.

Eight generated studies illustrate arrival, living, kitchen, material junction, bedroom, alfresco, cashmere and espresso/garnet directions. They are fictional inspiration, not approved WOLCO designs, completed projects, actual studio interiors or confirmed inclusions. The material tabs retain their names and use images matched to their visible finishes: Sand & stone → cashmere/mineral; Earth & ember → walnut/garnet; Oak & shadow → timber/charcoal kitchen. Images retain their natural tones without simulated dark-scheme filters.

Cookes Road photography remains supplied WOLCO project photography. The approximately 21.6-second, silent 1280×720/24fps film is edited from the supplied Cookes Road project film: Arrival, Entry, Volume, Gather, Craft, Restore and Home. Subtle contrast/saturation and dissolves support web presentation; architectural features and the original WOLCO corner mark remain.
