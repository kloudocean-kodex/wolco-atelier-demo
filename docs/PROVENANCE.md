## New generated studies

The exterior, kitchen, living and material studies were generated under the current user's explicit request. They are fictional visual inspiration. `docs/generation-prompts.json`, `docs/asset-manifest.json` and `docs/asset-verification.json` retain generation, derivative, dimension and verification evidence from the shared media task.

Public derivatives: each scene has a 1600×900 WebP plus 640px and 960px alternatives. Images are identified as architectural/design visualisations at the point of use. Alternate text and demonstration information also explain their status.

The final architectural film edits four genuine Gemini-generated videos made from the shared exterior, materials, kitchen and living references. Arrival → Touch → Gather → Dwell is joined by restrained 0.5-second dissolves. It contains continuous generated camera movement. The public version is 38.42 seconds, silent H.264, 1280×720 at 24fps, 922 frames and 12,104,844 bytes. Audio was removed and MP4 faststart applied. Complete source frames and visible model marks are retained. It opens deliberately and never autoplays. `docs/generated-architectural-journey-provenance.json` and the four individual clip records retain the source/output hashes, prompts, encoding and inspection evidence. Original downloads remain unchanged in the shared archive outside this deployment. The earlier two-clip record is retained as implementation history and is superseded by the final journey.
