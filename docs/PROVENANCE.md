## WOLCO exclusive generated studies

Eight architectural and selection studies were generated under the current user's explicit request: courtyard arrival, kitchen, living, material detail, bedroom, alfresco, cashmere and espresso/garnet. They are fictional visual inspiration. `docs/exclusive-media-manifest.json` records the current derivatives, classifications, bytes and hashes. The original generation records remain in the private source archive.

Public derivatives: each scene has a 1600×900 WebP plus 640px and 960px alternatives. Images are identified as architectural/design visualisations at the point of use. Alternate text and demonstration information also explain their status.

The current architectural film is a separate edit of genuine client-supplied WOLCO Cookes Road project footage. Arrival → Entry → Volume → Gather → Craft → Restore → Home is joined by 0.45-second dissolves. It is approximately 21.6 seconds, silent H.264, 1280×720 at 24fps and 5,290,007 bytes. A restrained grade supports consistent web presentation; original architectural features and the WOLCO corner mark remain. The film opens deliberately and never autoplays. `docs/wolco-project-journey-v2-provenance.json` records its source hash, edit and output. Superseded generated-film records are implementation history in `docs/history/pre-exclusive-media/`; no old generated movie remains public.

## Cookes Road project archive
